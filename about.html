The Art of Pure Stone Grinding

From rural roots in Nimdaha, Burdwan to modern Indian kitchens—our quest to eliminate adulteration.

The Problem with Modern Spices

In supermarket aisles today, spices have been industrialised. High-speed metal pulverizers grind seeds at temperatures exceeding 80°C. This extreme heat literally vaporizes the delicate, volatile essential oils—the exact elements responsible for aroma, digestion, and health benefits.

To compensate for the lost color and weight, mass-market brands often blend spent residues, sawdust, starch fillers, and artificial chemical dyes. At Ballab Masala, we refused to accept this decline in culinary integrity.

Our 4-Stage Traditional Process

1. Direct Sourcing: We source whole, unadulterated spice crops directly from vetted regional farmers in Bengal, Assam, and South India.

2. Sun-Drying: Moisture invites fungal decay. We sun-dry every batch under pristine solar conditions until optimum crispness is reached.

3. Slow Cold Stone Milling: Heavy traditional stone wheels grind the seeds slowly at room temperature, locking in aromatic terpenes.

4. Foil Hermetic Packaging: Spices are packaged inside food-grade airtight zip-pouches to prevent air from degrading the aroma.
