Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

At Ballab Masala, we respect your confidentiality just as strictly as we uphold spice purity. This Privacy Policy details how your personal details are handled during orders and interactions.

1. Zero-Database Privacy Architecture

Unlike conventional web portals that store names, phone numbers, and addresses in vulnerable internet-accessible SQL databases, Ballab Masala uses a serverless static architecture. Your order requests are encrypted and transmitted directly via WhatsApp to our official business fulfillment terminal. We do not sell, rent, or leak your credentials.

2. Information We Receive

When you place an order, we collect only necessary delivery parameters: your name, recipient delivery address, contact mobile number, and the specific spice varieties ordered.

3. Logistics & Delivery

Delivery information is shared solely with reputable postal and courier agencies (such as India Post or regional express delivery personnel) for the express purpose of dropping your package at your doorstep.
