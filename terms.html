1. Spice Purity & Allergen Notice

Ballab Masala guarantees 100% unadulterated raw spices free from foreign starch or artificial dyes. Our spices are processed in a dedicated facility. While pure, natural agricultural products may naturally trigger sensitivities in individuals with specific seed allergies.

2. High-Heat "Bullet Chilly" Precaution

Our "Bullet Chilly" variety contains significant Scoville heat levels. Customers are advised to handle it with care, avoid direct contact with eyes or sensitive skin, and keep it safely out of reach of infants and small children.

3. Returns & Replacements

Because spices are perishable culinary products, opened food pouches cannot be returned. However, if a package is damaged during transit or an incorrect spice was dispatched, message us via WhatsApp within 24 hours of delivery with a photo of the unboxing to receive an immediate free replacement.
