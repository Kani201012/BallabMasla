Our Heritage

Reviving the Lost Soul of Pure Stone Milling

Ballab Masala began with a simple disgust towards commercial mass-market spices, which often contain up to 30% foreign starch, spent seeds, and artificial yellow & red colorings.

We set up traditional stone-milling right in Nimdaha, Burdwan. Sourcing directly from trusted farms, sun-drying every seed under natural sunlight, and stone-grinding without rushing. The result is pure, vibrant, therapeutic spice that makes home-cooked dishes unforgettable.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our stone-milling purity and orders.

Are your spices 100% free from artificial food colorings?

Yes, absolutely. We do not use Sudan dyes, Lead Chromate, or synthetic color enhancers. The deep golden hue of our Turmeric and the striking red of our Assam Special Chilli come purely from natural carotenoids and curcumin.

What is the key difference between your 3 chilli powders?

Assam Special: Rich red hue, velvety texture, gentle mild warmth.
Fotki Tikha: Classic robust Bengali heat, pungent and zesty for daily curries.
Bullet Chilly: Intense, fiery Scoville potency for hardcore spice lovers.

How should I store stone-ground spices to maintain freshness?

Keep the spices in our resealable aroma-lock pouches or transfer them into airtight glass or stainless-steel jars away from direct sunlight and moisture. In these conditions, they will retain their full aromatic oils for 12 months.
